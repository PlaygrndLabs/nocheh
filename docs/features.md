# What Nocheh does

The full feature list, moved from the README. Setup is in the [README quick start](../README.md#quick-start).

## Features

- **Owns the source.** Every message, edit, reaction and file it sees is saved as an original, with where it came from, before any AI work starts. A slow or broken model never loses anything. Your messages and files are always kept. In **Settings › Privacy & data** you choose how long Nocheh keeps its own work records, such as work history.
- **Reads what you send.** Documents and photos are read into text and recordings are transcribed, live or imported, so you can search and ask about them. The text is checked for secrets like anything typed, and the original is kept.
- **Remembers people and projects.** Long-term memory (Honcho today) learns facts about people and projects, and each chat keeps its own assistant notes in Hermes. Who said something, and whether it was said, reported or inferred, stays attached.
- **Understands context.** It learns how a group works, like a ✅ reaction meaning "done", and follows the state of tasks and commitments even when it doesn't reply. You can inspect and correct what it learned.
- **Keeps your tasks.** Say "remind me to renew the domain on Friday" in your private chat, or use the **Tasks** page. Your personal list and each project's list stay apart, with due dates, repeats, reminders, a board view and your own columns. A project's tasks are one list that every group and topic of the project sees and changes, whatever "Share group chats" says, as its connections work; a task's group is only a label, and a group's tasks stay on the project's list when the group moves to another project. A reminder a member set in a group says who set it and where; theirs that come due together arrive as one message, at most one per group every 10 minutes, and they never remove yours.
- **Organizes knowledge.** Put related groups in one project for a shared role, skills and one task list; with your permission it can create projects, but putting a group in or out of one always asks you. In **Memory** you can look through people, projects and facts, and fix any of them.
- **Keeps audiences apart.** A group answer uses only that group, for everyone, you included. A project never shares one group's knowledge with another; only your own sharing rules and the facts you share let anything in, and they say who will see it. A project's task list is the exception: everyone in any of its groups, members or not, can see all of it. Under your own answer in a group of a project with other groups, **Answer privately** answers the same question in your private chat, reading that project's groups as they were when you tapped it. Your private chat can use everything.
- **Masks secrets.** A secret check finds passwords, keys and tokens and masks them before the assistant, memory or search ever sees them. You can review and edit every masked copy.
- **Asks before it acts.** Sending a message to another chat or running a shell, browser or MCP tool is proposed first, as a card with Approve and Reject. Nocheh sends only to your private chat, the groups you picked and their topics, and the private chats of your members; the card says which kind of chat it is and quotes its name. In a group, a web page read or an outside tool that you, or a member allowed web pages and outside tools, ask for runs without a card. Decide with one tap in Telegram, or in the Inbox of the dashboard or the mini app. On a card you can also allow a site or a command in that chat for a while, and revoke it later in **Inbox › Allowed actions**. A big background job gets the same card in Telegram, with its estimated cost. With **Do what I ask** in **Settings › Assistant**, messages and tools you ask for in your private chat run without a separate yes. A command still asks first when something you did not type yourself reached the conversation, such as a forward, a file, a voice note, a quoted reply, a web page or a search of your chats.
- **Lets you pick its tools per chat.** Turn **Commands**, **Web pages** and **Outside tools** on or off for every chat in **Settings › Assistant**, or for one chat or topic on its page in **Chats**. Nocheh checks these switches itself, whichever agent asks.
- **Forgets on request.** Hide any message from the assistant and Nocheh stops using it, including in what it learned. You can hide its replies to that message too. The original stays in your archive, and Undo hiding brings it back.
- **Meets you where you talk.** Today that is Telegram, privately and in the groups you pick, with your members there. Nocheh's own chat page in the dashboard comes later. On the **Members** page you add members, pause them, switch them off in a group and choose what each may do; every change applies at once. By default it answers in a group only when called: a reply to it, an @mention, or a message that starts with one of its names. It quietly saves everything else, and when called it also sees what was just said. Your private chat is yours alone: anyone else who writes to Nocheh privately gets one short fixed reply a day and never reaches a model. An optional Telegram mini app puts Home, Inbox, Tasks and Health on your phone.
- **Works on your own machines.** Add a VM or another machine you run in **Settings › Machines**, then ask for work there in chat. One plan card in your private chat covers the whole job. Nocheh keeps the machine's key, and the assistant never sees it.
- **Tells you when something breaks.** Your private chat gets a short notice about each new problem, such as failed work or a model that refused, with an **Open** button, plus **Retry failed** when failed work can run again. Turn notices off in **Settings › Telegram**.
- **A role and skills per project and chat.** Set the role and skills for every chat in **Settings › Assistant**, then give a project (in **Memory › Projects**) or a single chat (in **Chats**) its own. A chat's own role replaces its project's, which replaces the one for every chat. Skills add up. Only you can change them; the assistant can only update its notes.
- **Your models, your keys.** Use Anthropic, OpenAI or your own gateway (any OpenAI-compatible HTTPS address, with or without `/v1`). Pick one main model, or a different one for replies, memory, the secret check and images. Memory search and voice notes have their own choice. Keys stay on the server, replies on Anthropic use its prompt cache, and the Usage page shows what each part costs and how fast it answers.
- **Works with Notion.** Connect it and turn on **Use Notion** in **Connections › Yours**, and Nocheh can search, read and change your Notion from your private chat and from background jobs you approve there. By default reads run and changes ask you first, and groups never reach it. Notion counts as an outside tool, so **Outside tools** must be on for that chat.
- **Reads your Gmail.** Make a Google Cloud project in Personal mode, paste its keys in **Connections › Yours › Your Gmail**, and press **Connect Gmail**. Nocheh can then search your mail and read a message or a conversation, only in your private chat and read only.
- **Instructions you can edit.** In **Settings › Assistant** you can edit Nocheh's instructions to models and the fixed texts it sends, such as approval cards, reminders and the lines that say who set a reminder and where, problem notices and the reply to strangers. Each one can be reset to its default.
- **Yours to take with you.** Import chat history (Telegram Desktop exports today). Export your archive, memory and your own work, such as tasks, sharing rules and settings, in a portable format. Back up or restore the whole installation. Backups run on their own every day, and encrypted copies can go to storage you choose, off this machine.

## Use it

- **Ask what you know.** "What did Sara say about the launch?" or "Sum up last week in the design group." Nocheh answers from your archive and memory, within what that chat is allowed to see. It can read a whole chat in order, or just between two dates. In chats that long-term memory has learned from, it also finds messages by meaning.
- **Let it follow along.** Leave it in a group and let people talk. Later, ask "What's still open in the design group?" and it answers from what it followed.
- **Ask it to act.** By default, "Tell the design group the meeting moved to 4" becomes a proposal you approve before anything is sent.
- **Hand it big work.** Ask for something too big for one reply, like "read the whole design chat and list every decision", and Nocheh offers a background job with its estimated time and cost. Once it is approved, the job runs on its own and sends its report to that chat. Watch or stop it in **System › Background work**, or stop it with Stop on its card. A job you ask for in your private chat can also run commands and Python in a sealed box with no internet and no keys, and read a copy of a GitHub repository you make in **Connections › GitHub app**; turn that off with **Sealed box for jobs** in **Settings › Assistant**.
- **Put it to work on a machine.** Ask from your private chat or a project's group, like "set up the demo project on build-vm". Nocheh sends one plan card to your private chat with the machine, the steps, the time and the cost. Once you approve it, the job works on that machine over SSH for up to 50 minutes. **System › Machine log** lists every command it ran, and Stop ends only the programs that job started there. Nocheh can use the login you use yourself, and your own programs keep running.
- **Bring in your history.** Import old chats in **Messages › Import**. Before you start, it shows what checking and learning will cost, and you choose whether long-term memory may learn from them. Every message is saved, read and checked for secrets, and your private chat gets a message when the import is ready to ask about.
- **Look inside its head.** The dashboard shows what it knows and why. **Home** shows status, **Inbox** shows what waits on you, and **Tasks** your task lists. **Chats** shows who can see what and which tools each chat may use, **Messages** every original and your imports, and **Memory** people, projects, facts, notes and which memory engine is plugged in. **Usage** shows cost and speed, **System** health, background work, the machine log and backups, and **Settings** models, Telegram, connections, machines and every instruction.
- **Correct it.** Fix a fact, rename a person, hide a message or undo an organization change. Nocheh rebuilds the affected memory from what remains.

## Good to know

- Nocheh answers only while its services are running. Telegram holds new messages for up to 24 hours, so short breaks lose nothing there.
- Dependable answers come before speed. A reply usually takes around half a minute, a bit longer when it first reads a new file, photo or voice note. While Nocheh works on it, Telegram shows "typing…" and the bot puts 👀 on the message it answers, then 👍 when it answered or 👎 when it failed.
- Photos are read by the **Images** model, which is your main model unless you pick another. Voice notes, audio files and round video notes are transcribed by the **Voice notes** model, OpenAI by default. Both are in **Settings › Models**. Recordings saved before you add a key are transcribed once you do. Stickers, animations and other videos are kept as originals only.
- Nocheh backs up the archive, memory and settings every day and keeps the newest seven automatic copies by default. Change the schedule in **Settings › Privacy & data**. Backups stay on this machine unless you turn on **Off-site copies** there: each new backup is then encrypted with your passphrase and sent to storage you choose: Cloudflare R2 connects from one pasted token, Backblaze B2 from one key, and any other S3-compatible storage from its address and keys. Keep the passphrase somewhere else, because without it no copy can be opened. Machine keys are left out of backups, so after a restore you add each machine again.
- No local models. Replies, learning, the secret check, images, memory search and voice notes all run on model providers you pay.
- Nocheh is in development and not released yet. Live release checks are tracked in [mvp-acceptance-status.md](mvp-acceptance-status.md).
